# Design Services Agreement

Between Northlight Studio ("Northlight") and Fleetwise Inc. ("Fleetwise").
It records what Ana Ruiz, Marcus Okafor and Priya Shah agreed on their call on October 14, 2026, and nothing else.

## 1. Scope

**1.1**
Northlight will design four flows of the Fleetwise driver app: onboarding, route view, proof of delivery and shift handover.
The aim is to cut drop-off in driver onboarding and make daily routes easier to follow.

**1.2**
The work runs for eight weeks from the start date.
At handover Northlight delivers the final design files and a clickable prototype of each flow.

**1.3**
Adding shift handover does not change the fee or the timeline.

## 2. Fees and payment

**2.1**
The fee is $48,000.
It covers all of the design work in clause 1, and there are no other charges unless both sides agree them in writing.

**2.2**
Fleetwise pays half of the fee at the start date.
Fleetwise pays the other half within thirty days of handover.

## 3. Ownership

**3.1**
Northlight owns all designs, files and source materials created under this agreement.
Northlight grants Fleetwise a non-exclusive license to use the final designs in the driver app.

**3.2**
Northlight keeps its own tools, templates and component library.
Northlight uses them for every client, and they are not part of the final designs.

**3.3**
Northlight may show the work in its portfolio once the driver app is publicly available, and not before.

## 4. Working together

**4.1**
Each side names one day-to-day contact.
The contacts make everyday decisions and keep the project moving.
For Fleetwise, the contact is Priya Shah, Head of Product.

**4.2**
Northlight sends Fleetwise a written progress update every Friday, covering work done, work planned and any open questions.

**4.3**
During the term and for eighteen months after it ends, Northlight will not provide design services to any company that offers delivery, courier or fleet management software.
Northlight will tell Fleetwise in writing before it accepts any engagement that may fall under this clause.

## 5. Confidentiality

**5.1**
Each side keeps the other's confidential information private and uses it only for this project.
This duty lasts for two years after the agreement ends and does not cover information that is already public.

## 6. Ending the agreement

**6.1**
Fleetwise may end this agreement at any time with 14 days' written notice.
Northlight may end this agreement only if Fleetwise fails to pay an undisputed invoice within sixty days of its due date.

**6.2**
Fleetwise pays for work done up to the end date.
On the end date Northlight hands over all work in progress.

**6.3**
This agreement renews automatically for successive twelve-month terms after the initial eight weeks.
During each renewal term Fleetwise pays Northlight a monthly retainer of $6,000 for ongoing design support.
Either side may stop a renewal by giving sixty days' written notice before the renewal date.

## 7. Liability

**7.1**
Neither side is liable for indirect, incidental or consequential losses, including lost profits.

**7.2**
Each side's total liability under this agreement is capped at twice the fees paid under it.

## 8. Definitions

**8.1**
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information one side shares with the other in connection with this agreement, in any form.

**8.2**
"Deliverables" means the final design files for the flows listed in clause 1.1, in the formats agreed by the day-to-day contacts.

**8.3**
"Handover" means the date on which Northlight delivers the Deliverables and Fleetwise confirms in writing that it has received them.

**8.4**
"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in the State of New York.

## 9. Notices

**9.1**
Notices under this agreement must be in writing and sent by email to the day-to-day contact, with a copy to each side's address on file.

**9.2**
A notice sent by email is received on the next Business Day after it is sent, unless the sender receives an automated message that it was not delivered.

**9.3**
Either side may change its notice details by giving notice to the other side.

## 10. Governing law and disputes

**10.1**
This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

**10.2**
Before starting any formal proceedings, the day-to-day contacts will meet within ten Business Days of a written request to try to resolve the dispute in good faith.

**10.3**
If the dispute is not resolved within thirty days of that meeting, either side may bring proceedings in the state or federal courts located in New York County, and each side submits to their jurisdiction.

## 11. Force majeure

**11.1**
Neither side is liable for a delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, war, civil unrest, government action, labor disputes not involving its own staff, and failures of public utilities or networks.

**11.2**
The affected side must tell the other side promptly, describe the event and its likely effect, and use reasonable efforts to keep performing.

**11.3**
If the event continues for more than thirty days, either side may end this agreement by notice, and Fleetwise pays for work done up to the end date.
